# Founder Thesis

## Key Activities

### Problem Statement
Most SaaS founders waste months building products that nobody wants. They start with an idea-first mindset instead of validating the problem, leading to failure before their first paying customer.

### Why Me (Founder–Problem Fit)
- I’ve personally experienced multiple failed projects due to lack of market validation.
- My skills in lean product development and AI tooling give me leverage to solve this faster than others.

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Solo founders, indie hackers, early SaaS teams (1–3 people).
- Aged 22–35, tech or marketing background.
- Global, but concentrated in US/EU/SEA startup communities.
- Goal: launch SaaS and make the first $1–$100 revenue within 30–60 days.

### Job-to-be-Done (JTBD)
> “When I have a SaaS idea, help me validate the problem, build a thin-slice MVP, and close my first paying customer — without wasting months coding the wrong thing.”

### Key Hypotheses
- Early founders want a clear, structured framework to avoid “building in the dark.”
- They are willing to pay $9–29/month if it helps them get to first revenue faster.
- Reddit, Twitter, and niche founder communities are effective acquisition channels.
- AI-assisted workflows can dramatically shorten validation and MVP cycles.

### Constraints
- **Time:** Must achieve first paying customer within 30 days.
- **Capital:** <$200 budget for tools, hosting, and test ads.
- **Team:** Solo founder; must rely heavily on automation and AI agents.

### North Star Metric
One paying customer outside my personal network.
